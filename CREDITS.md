# Credits and licenses

Icy Tower: Roadster Edition is an **unofficial, non-commercial fan edition**. It is free, has no ads, no tracking and no donations, and is never bundled or sold.

## Icy Tower — © Johan Peitz / Free Lunch Design

Icy Tower 1.3 was written, designed and drawn by **Johan Peitz**; its music and sound effects are by **Anders Svensson**. Icy Tower is freeware by **Free Lunch Design**: <https://www.freelunchdesign.com>

The original readme asks, under DISTRIBUTING:

> "Our only requirement is that you give proper credits to Free Lunch Design and provide a link back to our site. However, this game may not be included in a compilation or any other kind of commercial package without the permission from the author."

This edition credits Free Lunch Design and links back on every page of the site, in this repository and in the release notes, and is never sold or bundled.

Everything in this edition that comes from Icy Tower stays © Johan Peitz / Free Lunch Design and is **not** covered by our licenses. That includes the tower graphics, Harold, the fonts, music, sound effects and game data, and the gameplay rules that the native rebuild reproduces. It also covers the Icy Tower material visible in the gameplay video and screenshots.

If you are from Free Lunch Design and want anything changed or taken down, please [open an issue](https://github.com/fire17/icy-tower-roadster/issues) and it will be done.
